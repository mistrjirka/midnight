## Simpler Midnight menu and photo Live View gain

- Eight quick controls fit on one screen. Adds **Live View gain**, Off to **+6 EV (64x)**. RIGHT raises and LEFT lowers the gain by one stop, without wrapping at either end.
- Reuses ML's existing photo-preview control, shared with **Display -> LV display gain**. No second gain setting or custom register writes. ISO, saved RAW/JPEG exposure and movie digital ISO are not changed. Gain also amplifies noise and clips highlights, so a boosted preview is not an exposure guide.
- Gain changes are blocked in movie mode and while a Midnight focus/solve task is running.
- Moves one-shot focus, motor tuning and shortcut choices into **Midnight advanced -> Star focus settings**. Moves read-only coordinates into **Last solve** and PGM file tests into **Developer tests**.
- Original photo/video/audio/debug features remain available under **Full Magic Lantern**. No camera settings are removed or reset.

Native album decoding and physical lenses/cameras remain unverified.
